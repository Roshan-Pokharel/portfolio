About Me

Hello! I'm Roshan Pokhrel, a Full Stack and Mobile App Developer currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology at Butwal Multiple Campus. My fascination with technology started when I built my first simple webpage and saw it come to life, and it has since grown into a passion for full-cycle software architecture.

I specialize in building robust web and mobile experiences. Along with foundational languages like C++ and Java, my core tech stack revolves around the modern JavaScript ecosystem. I am highly proficient in React and Next.js for the frontend, Node.js and Express for scalable backend REST APIs, and React Native for cross-platform mobile app development — backed by MongoDB, MySQL, and PostgreSQL on the data side.

Outside of coding, I enjoy drawing, reading, and fishing. I am a collaborative problem-solver, continuously expanding my technical repertoire, and currently open to full-time roles and professional opportunities in software engineering.
